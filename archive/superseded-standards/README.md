## Contents

### `TCCS_PUBLIC_STANDARD_v1_SUPERSEDED.md`
- **Original self-description:** "TCCS/1.0/2026 — Status: Active — first published
  standard — Issued by The Clean Sheet Standards Council."
- **Archived on:** 21 July 2026
- **Why superseded:** Later research (the Product Standards Framework 2026, Audit
  Notes sheet) identified critical, safety-relevant problems with this draft's core
  logic. In particular it relied on:
  - a "stricter than any single national regulation" strictest-rule principle that
    combines numerically strictest limits across jurisdictions with different scope,
    exposure and legal definitions — producing scientifically incoherent outcomes;
  - blanket IARC / hazard-class bans used as product-level decisions;
  - universal allergen and threshold rules applied across all markets regardless of
    exposure;
  - a weighted score able to mask a failed critical requirement.
- **Status:** Superseded internal draft. Replaced in direction by the modular,
  gate-based **Product Standards Framework 2026 (v0.9, under validation)**.

This document was never validated by independent experts and must not be represented
as approved by a Standards Council.
