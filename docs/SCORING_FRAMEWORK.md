# The Clean Sheet - Internal Scoring Framework

> **Internal use only. Do not publish point breakdowns or sub-scores publicly.**
> Public scorecards show pillar names, performance bars, and qualitative notes only.
> All scores are calculated exclusively from publicly available data (INCI lists, brand claims, regulatory databases, peer-reviewed literature). Nothing is fabricated or assumed.

---

## Our Approach to Ingredient Assessment

The Clean Sheet does not use fear-based ingredient labels. We assess products through a structured evidence hierarchy:

1. What global regulations say
2. What toxicology says
3. What the formula concentration shows
4. What the product format changes
5. What the intended user needs
6. What testing evidence proves
7. What the brand is claiming

Every concern flagged in a scorecard is grounded in at least one of these layers. A concern raised only by a watchdog list, without regulatory action, peer-reviewed toxicology, or concentration evidence at cosmetic use levels, is not sufficient grounds for a warning on The Clean Sheet.

## Score Tiers

| Score | Label      | Description                                                                       |
|-------|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 90–100 | Excellent  | Exemplary formulation. No meaningful safety, transparency, or ethics concerns.   |
| 75–89  | Good       | Above-average. Minor gaps that do not materially affect safety or efficacy.       |
| 60–74  | Fair       | Acceptable but notable concerns consumers should be aware of.                     |
| Below 60 | Concern  | Significant ingredient, transparency, or regulatory issues identified.            |

## Scoring Architecture

The Clean Sheet score is the sum of four evaluated pillars, totalling 100 points.

Layer 1 (Legal Compliance) is a hard pass/fail gate evaluated before scoring begins. Any product containing an EU Annex II prohibited substance, an undisclosed fragrance allergen above threshold, or making drug claims without regulatory approval receives an automatic score floor of 0 regardless of other pillar performance.

---

## Pillar 1 — Safety & Toxicity (50 points)

The highest-weighted pillar because consumer safety is the non-negotiable foundation.

### A. Ingredient Hazard Assessment (25 pts)

Evaluated against SCCS (Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety) opinions, EU Annex II/III/V, FDA 21 CFR, IARC classifications, and REACH candidate list:

- **Carcinogenicity** (5 pts): Does any ingredient have IARC Group 1 or 2A/2B classification at cosmetic use concentrations?
- **Mutagenicity/Genotoxicity** (5 pts): Any ingredient flagged under EU CMR categories or Ames test data?
- **Reproductive & Developmental Toxicity** (5 pts): Evaluated per EU Regulation 1223/2009 Category 1A/1B/2 CMR substances.
- **Endocrine Disruption** (5 pts): Evaluated against EU SVHC candidate list, REACH endocrine disruptors list, and peer-reviewed in-vitro/in-vivo data (e.g., Benzophenone-3, Parabens with estrogenic activity).
- **Organ/Systemic Toxicity** (5 pts): Evaluated for repeat-dose toxicity at cosmetic exposure levels using SCCS Guidance Document on the Safety Assessment of Cosmetic Ingredients.

### B. Exposure Assessment (15 pts)

- **Product Type Factor** (5 pts): Leave-on products receive stricter evaluation than rinse-off. Overnight leave-on receives the highest scrutiny (8+ hours continuous contact). Treatment/weekly-use products are evaluated at actual exposure frequency.
- **Concentration Limit Compliance** (5 pts): Evaluated against EU Annex III/V permitted concentrations, IFRA category limits for fragrance materials, and India BIS/CDSCO Schedule M.
- **Aggregate Daily Exposure** (5 pts): Estimated systemic exposure based on typical use quantity, body surface area covered, and product type (SCCS methodology).

### C. Sensitisation & Irritation (10 pts)

- **Skin Sensitisation Potential** (5 pts): Evaluated against ECHA classification, LLNA (Local Lymph Node Assay) data, and published contact dermatitis literature. Fragrance allergens above EU threshold (0.001% leave-on / 0.01% rinse-off) require disclosure and receive deductions.
- **Eye Irritation Potential** (3 pts): Evaluated for periocular products and products likely to contact eyes.
- **Respiratory Irritation** (2 pts): Evaluated for spray/aerosol products.

### Auto-Fail Triggers (Layer Safety)

Any of the following sets Layer 1 to FAIL and zeroes the score:
- Ingredient banned under EU Annex II
- SVHC above 0.1% w/w without exemption
- Undisclosed fragrance allergens above regulatory threshold
- IFRA standard exceeded for product category
- Formaldehyde-releasing preservative in a baby/children's product

## Pillar 2 — Formulation Quality (20 points)

Evaluates whether the formula is well-designed, stable, and substantiated. Scored from publicly available information (brand transparency disclosures, published studies, INCI logic analysis, packaging review).

### A. Claim Substantiation (8 pts)

Evaluated against published peer-reviewed literature, brand-provided clinical data, and plausibility of INCI-position-based concentration analysis:

- **Evidence exists for key claims** (4 pts): Is there published in-vivo or in-vitro evidence for the primary claim at the concentration indicated by INCI position?
- **Evidence from credible sources** (3 pts): SCCS opinions, Cochrane reviews, British Journal of Dermatology, JAAD, peer-reviewed pharmacology journals.
- **Formulation logic supports claims** (1 pt): Does INCI position order (descending by concentration) place key actives at functional concentrations?

### B. Formulation Stability (6 pts)

Evaluated from public information:

- **Microbiological stability** (2 pts): Is preservative system appropriate for the formula's pH and water activity? Is preservative challenge testing referenced?
- **Chemical/physical stability** (2 pts): Are pH-sensitive actives (Vitamin C, retinol) in appropriate vehicles or packaging (amber glass, anhydrous)?
- **Packaging compatibility** (2 pts): Does packaging protect against oxidation, light, contamination?

### C. Manufacturing Quality Signal (6 pts)

Evaluated from publicly available information only. No score is assigned for data not publicly verifiable.

- **GMP compliance evidence** (3 pts): Brand's manufacturing facility registration under India CDSCO Schedule M, ISO 22716, or equivalent. Large multinationals with publicly confirmed GMP: 3/3. Indian indie brands with CDSCO registration: 2/3. No public manufacturing information: 1/3.
- **Stability and testing transparency** (2 pts): Does brand publish stability data, SPF testing methods, or clinical study references?
- **Batch traceability evidence** (1 pt): Does brand provide lot number, manufacture date, and expiry date clearly?

---

## Pillar 3 — Claims & Transparency (20 points)

Evaluates completeness of disclosure and accuracy of marketing communications. Evaluated from brand website, product page, packaging, and INCI list.

### A. INCI Disclosure Completeness (10 pts)

- **All ingredients listed in correct INCI format** (5 pts): Are all ingredients named by their INCI name (not trade names, not common names)? Are all ingredients present in the product listed?
- **Active concentration ranges provided** (3 pts): Does the brand disclose key active concentrations (e.g., "Niacinamide 10%", "Retinol 0.3%")?
- **Fragrance components disclosed** (2 pts): Are fragrance allergens above threshold explicitly named in INCI (not just "Parfum")? Are constituent essential oil allergens listed separately?

### B. Labelling Clarity (6 pts)

- **Correct INCI names used** (2 pts): No use of trade names, common names, or marketing-adjacent ingredient naming in the INCI list.
- **Language accessibility** (2 pts): Is consumer-facing information (ingredient function, usage guidance, warnings) communicated clearly?
- **Allergen highlighting** (2 pts): Are allergens, contraindications (e.g., pregnancy for retinol), and skin type cautions prominently communicated?

### C. Marketing Claims Accuracy (4 pts)

- **No prohibited or misleading terms** (2 pts): No "chemical-free," "toxin-free," "natural" without qualification, or "dermatologist-approved" without specifying the scope.
- **Claims match ingredient profile** (2 pts): Does the marketed benefit (e.g., "retinol night cream," "vitamin C serum") accurately reflect the active ingredient as declared in INCI? Retinyl Palmitate is NOT Retinol; Ascorbyl Glucoside is NOT L-Ascorbic Acid — if marketed equivalently, deductions apply.

### Auto-Fail Triggers (Layer Claims)

- Drug claims without regulatory approval (e.g., treating acne as a disease claim)
- "Chemical-free" or scientifically incoherent claims
- SPF claim without validated SPF test data
- "Organic" / "natural" / "vegan" claim without supporting evidence or certification

## Pillar 4 — Ethics & Sustainability (10 points)

### A. Cruelty-Free Status (3 pts)

- No finished product animal testing (1.5 pts): Brand does not conduct or commission animal testing on finished cosmetics.
- No ingredient animal testing (1 pt): Where alternatives exist, animal testing for ingredients is not conducted.
- Not sold in mandatory-testing markets (0.5 pts): Brand does not sell in markets requiring pre-market animal testing (mainland China for imported cosmetics, as of 2021 reform — note that general cosmetics reformulation allows e-filing from 2021, but brands not yet adapted receive deduction).

### B. Ingredient Ethics (3 pts)

- Natural origin index (1.5 pts): Proportion of ingredients from natural or renewable origin (COSMOS standard as reference). Scaled: ≥95% = 1.5; 75–94% = 1.0; 50–74% = 0.5; below 50% = 0.
- Not on COSMOS prohibited list (1 pt): No use of ingredients prohibited by COSMOS Organic/Natural standard (silicones, PEGs, synthetic fragrance, synthetic dyes).
- No synthetic fragrance, PEG, or silicone in leave-on (0.5 pts): Leave-on products free from all three classes score 0.5.

### C. Ethical Sourcing (2 pts)

- RSPO-certified palm oil / palm derivatives (1 pt): Brand or parent company is RSPO member or uses RSPO-certified palm derivatives.
- Mica sourcing verified (0.5 pts): For products containing mica or iron oxides, is sourcing verified (Responsible Mica Initiative membership or equivalent)?
- No conflict minerals (0.5 pts): Verified absence of conflict-source minerals in pigments.

### D. Packaging Sustainability (2 pts)

- Recyclable or refillable primary packaging (1 pt): Primary container is glass, aluminium, or widely recyclable plastic (PET, HDPE).
- Post-consumer recycled content ≥30% (0.5 pts): Packaging uses measurable recycled content.
- Minimal plastic secondary packaging (0.5 pts): Outer box/secondary packaging is paper-based, minimal, or absent.

## Special Evaluation Rules

- **Leave-on products** receive stricter sensitisation evaluation (thresholds apply at 0.001% for fragrance allergens vs 0.01% for rinse-off).
- **Sunscreens** require SPF validation and photostability data for the UV filter system. Filters not listed on EU/India approved lists receive deduction.
- **Treatment products** (weekly peels, masks) are evaluated at actual use frequency, not daily.
- **Fragrance allergens** disclosed as required by EU Regulation 1223/2009 Annex III receive transparency credit. Undisclosed allergens are an auto-fail trigger.
- **Penetration enhancers** (Dimethyl Isosorbide, DGME, Ethoxydiglycol) present at high INCI positions receive an exposure assessment deduction because they increase systemic absorption of co-formulated actives.

## Skin Type Guidance (Required on all Product Scorecards)

Every scorecard must include at least one skin type badge and one contextual note:

| Badge | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| Sensitive Skin Friendly | Fragrance-free, no essential oils, no dyes, allantoin or Centella present |
| Oily / Acne-Prone Skin | Non-comedogenic, zinc or salicylic acid present, lightweight texture |
| Dry Skin Friendly | Rich emollient base, ceramides or occlusive present |
| Combination Skin | Balanced formula: humectants + lightweight emollients |
| All Skin Types | Minimal actives, universally tolerated vehicle (e.g., water + glycerin base) |
| Not Recommended for Sensitive Skin | Fragrance, essential oils, or high-allergen load present |
| Caution: Acne-Prone | Comedogenic potential from fatty esters at high INCI position |

## Consumer Summary Requirement

Each product scorecard must include a consumer-facing summary that:

1. Opens with one sentence explaining what the product is and who it is for.
2. Calls out the most important positive (best ingredient, best formulation feature).
3. Calls out the most important concern (if any) in plain language.
4. Ends with a practical recommendation (good for X, use with Y, avoid if Z).

Technical terms must be explained parenthetically on first use. The summary must be understandable to someone with no chemistry background.

## Data Sources (All Publicly Available)

- Ingredient safety data: SCCS opinions (ec.europa.eu/health/scientific_committees), CosIng database, EWG Skin Deep, ECHA

- Ethics/sustainability: RSPO (rspo.org), Leaping Bunny (leapingbunny.org), Responsible Mica Initiative (responsiblemicainitiative.com)
